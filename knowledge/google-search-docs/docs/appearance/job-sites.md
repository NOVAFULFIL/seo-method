# Job sites: aggregator carousel and refinement chip

This page describes features for job sites in Google Search, including the aggregator carousel and refinement chips for job-related queries in [European Economic Area (EEA)](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Glossary%3AEuropean_Economic_Area_%28EEA%29) countries. Publishers don't need to add any markup to be eligible for the aggregator carousel or refinement chips.

![Job sites aggregator carousel and refinement chip in Google Search](/static/search/docs/images/job-sites.png)

The "Job sites" aggregator carousel lets users easily see the top aggregator results for their query. Users can also click **More sites** to see additional relevant aggregator sites for their query.

The "Job sites" refinement chip can help users focus the Search page on aggregator text results. Searchers can activate the refinement chips at the top of the Search results page or by clicking **More sites** on the aggregator carousel.

## Feature availability

These experiences are available to users in the [EEA](https://ec.europa.eu/eurostat/statistics-explained/index.php?title=Glossary%3AEuropean_Economic_Area_%28EEA%29) who are searching for job-related queries.

## Learn more and express interest

If your business serves users in the EEA and you want to learn more and express interest in these experiences, you can start by filling out the [interest form](https://support.google.com/websearch/contact/search_aggregator_features).
